**Date:** 3 November 2026. **Client:** A15 / `raft-20261103T123140Z-7ea78212`. **Issue:** RENDER-002, bounded diagnostic correctness only.

## Why this matters

The incomplete [native preview41624581951](https://github.com/alwansan/raf/actions/runs/41624581951) ran `scripts.audit_render_input_contracts` before Unity, reporting `shaders=140`, `source_pass_histogram={1:140}` and `computes=0`. Later the build stopped at strict `No exported built-in shader identities matched`. The historical report was generated by raw lexical `Pass\s*\{`, `CGPROGRAM|HLSLPROGRAM`, `#pragma kernel`, and `Shader` regular expressions unaware of comments or quoted text. A string such as a ShaderLab shader name containing `Pass {` or a block comment with `#pragma kernel` could therefore change the reported pass/kernel count without changing any executable shader declarations.

**Crucial distinction:** the original140-shader export was not available for this source-only test. This does *not* establish that its pass histogram was wrong or that this parser issue caused the built-in identity failure. The original 107-file local sample is not authenticated as the failed run's complete export either.
